\begin{abstract}
A conforming finite element discretization for a nonlocal elliptic operator is proposed and analyzed. Under minimal regularity assumptions we derive optimal-order error estimates in the energy norm.
\end{abstract}
\paragraph{Keywords} finite element method; nonlocal operator; a priori error estimate; convergence
