\begin{abstract}
We study a reaction--diffusion equation with a nonlocal competition
term. Existence of a global attractor in $H^1_0(\Om)$ and upper bounds
for its fractal dimension are obtained.

\section{Introduction}

Let $\Om\subset\R^n$ be bounded and consider
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rd}
  u_t - \Delta u = u\Bigl(1 - \int_\Om K(x,y)u(y,t)\,\dd y\Bigr),
  \qquad u|_{\partial\Om}=0 .
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}
For a nonnegative bounded kernel $K$, problem \eqref{eq:rd} generates a
dissipative semigroup on $H^1_0(\Om)$ possessing a compact global
attractor $\mathcal{A}$ with finite fractal dimension.
\end{theorem}

\section{Proof outline}
Uniform $L^\infty$ bounds follow from the maximum principle; the
smoothing effect of the analytic semigroup gives compactness, and the
dimension estimate follows from the trace formula.
