\begin{abstract}
We prove weighted norm inequalities for Calderon-Zygmund operators on Morrey spaces associated with Muckenhoupt weights.
\end{abstract}
\paragraph{Keywords} singular integral; Morrey space; Muckenhoupt weight; weighted norm inequality

\section{Introduction}
Let $w\in A_p$ be a Muckenhoupt weight and let $T$ be a standard Calderon-Zygmund operator with cancellation and size estimates.
For the classical background and related results, see~\cite{Evans,Brezis}.

\begin{theorem}
For $1<p<\infty$ and $0<\lambda<n$, $T$ is bounded on the weighted Morrey space $L^{p,\lambda}(w)$ with norm depending only on $[w]_{A_p}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The sharp maximal function control and a weighted Fefferman-Stein inequality reduce the estimate to the weighted $L^p$ theory.
\end{proof}
