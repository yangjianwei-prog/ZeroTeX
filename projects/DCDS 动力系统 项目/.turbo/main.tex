\section{Introduction}
On a bounded domain $\Om\subset\R^n$ consider $u_{tt}-\mathrm{div}(a(x)\nabla u)+b(x)u_t=0$ with Dirichlet boundary data.
For the classical background and related results, see~\cite{Evans,Brezis}.

\begin{theorem}
If the damping region satisfies the geometric control condition, then $E(t)\le C(1+t)^{-1}\,E(0)$ for all $t\ge0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A resolvent estimate combined with a multiplier identity yields the polynomial decay via the semigroup interpolation argument.
\end{proof}
